# Calculating Gross Margin in Excel

## Overview

Gross profit is the revenue remaining after deducting the cost of goods sold (COGS).

Gross margin percentage shows how much gross profit a business generates for every pound of net revenue.

## 1. Calculate Gross Profit

**Formula:**

Gross Profit = Net Revenue − Total COGS

For example, if net revenue is in cell `B9` and total COGS is in cell `B16`, enter:

`=B9-B16`

This calculates gross profit for the first forecast year.

## 2. Calculate Gross Margin Percentage

**Formula:**

Gross Margin % = Gross Profit / Net Revenue

If gross profit is in `B17` and net revenue is in `B9`, enter:

`=B17/B9`

Format the result as a percentage.

### Example

Suppose a company has:

- Net revenue: £100,000
- COGS: £60,000
- Gross profit: £40,000

Gross margin percentage:

£40,000 / £100,000 = 40%

The company retains 40p in gross profit for every £1 of net revenue before operating expenses.

## 3. Copy Formulas Across Forecast Years

Use Excel's fill handle to copy the formulas across the remaining forecast years.

Excel automatically adjusts relative references.

For example:

- Year 1: `=B9-B16`
- Year 2: `=C9-C16`
- Year 3: `=D9-D16`

Repeat the process for gross margin percentage.

## 4. Formatting

- Format gross margin percentage as a percentage.
- Use a top border to separate gross profit from the calculations above it.
- Consider using a double bottom border for key totals.
- Keep formatting consistent across all forecast years.

## Key Takeaways

- Gross profit equals net revenue minus COGS.
- Gross margin percentage equals gross profit divided by net revenue.
- Copying formulas across makes forecasts easier to maintain.
- Gross margin helps assess the profitability of a company's products or services before operating expenses.
