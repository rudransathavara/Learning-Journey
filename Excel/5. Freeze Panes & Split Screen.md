# Freeze Panes and Split Screen

## 1. Split Screen

The **Split** feature allows you to divide your worksheet into separate sections so you can view different parts of the same worksheet at once.

### How to Split the Screen

1. Open the **View** tab in the Excel ribbon.
2. Find the **Window** section.
3. Click **Split**.
4. Excel will display dividing lines across your worksheet.
5. Drag these lines to adjust the size of each section.

You can split your screen horizontally, vertically, or both, allowing you to view different areas of your worksheet simultaneously.

### Why Use Split Screen?

- View the top and bottom of a large worksheet at the same time.
- View the left and right sides of a worksheet simultaneously.
- Compare information in different sections without constantly scrolling back and forth.

### Removing the Split

To remove the split, go back to **View → Split** and click it again.

## 2. Freeze Panes

The **Freeze Panes** feature keeps specific rows or columns visible while you scroll through the rest of your worksheet.

This is especially useful when working with large datasets because you can keep headings or important information on screen.

### Freezing the Top Row

If you want your column headings to remain visible while scrolling down:

1. Open the **View** tab.
2. Find the **Freeze Panes** option.
3. Click **Freeze Top Row**.

The top row will remain visible as you scroll down the worksheet.

### Freezing the First Column

If you want the first column to remain visible while scrolling horizontally:

1. Open the **View** tab.
2. Click **Freeze Panes**.
3. Select **Freeze First Column**.

The first column will remain visible as you scroll to the right.

### Freezing Specific Rows and Columns

You can freeze multiple rows and columns at the same time by selecting a cell before applying Freeze Panes.

**Important rule:** Excel freezes the rows above the selected cell and the columns to the left of it.

For example, if you select cell **C3** and then choose **Freeze Panes → Freeze Panes**:

- Rows 1 and 2 remain visible when scrolling vertically.
- Columns A and B remain visible when scrolling horizontally.

To do this:

1. Select the cell below the rows and to the right of the columns you want to keep visible.
2. Open the **View** tab.
3. Click **Freeze Panes**.
4. Select **Freeze Panes** from the menu.

### Unfreezing Panes

To remove frozen rows or columns:

1. Open the **View** tab.
2. Click **Freeze Panes**.
3. Select **Unfreeze Panes**.

Your worksheet will return to normal scrolling.

---

## 3. Difference Between Split and Freeze Panes

| Feature | Split | Freeze Panes |
|---|---|---|
| Main purpose | Divides the worksheet into separate viewing sections | Keeps selected rows or columns visible while scrolling |
| Can view different areas simultaneously | Yes | Yes, by keeping selected areas visible alongside the scrolling area |
| Can adjust dividing lines | Yes | No, the frozen area is determined by the selected rows and columns |
| Useful for | Comparing different sections of a worksheet | Keeping headings or identifying columns visible |

## Key Takeaways

- **Split** divides the worksheet into separate sections so you can view different areas at the same time.
- You can adjust the split sections by dragging the dividing lines.
- **Freeze Top Row** keeps the top row visible while scrolling vertically.
- **Freeze First Column** keeps the first column visible while scrolling horizontally.
- **Freeze Panes** lets you keep specific rows and columns visible.
- The selected cell determines which rows and columns are frozen.
- **Unfreeze Panes** removes frozen rows and columns.
