# Forecasting Fixed Assets and Accumulated Depreciation in Excel

## Overview

Fixed assets and accumulated depreciation are important components of a balance sheet forecast.

The calculations connect the balance sheet to the CapEx and depreciation schedule.

## 1. Forecast Gross Fixed Assets

A simplified formula is:

Closing Gross Fixed Assets = Opening Gross Fixed Assets + CapEx

Suppose:
- Opening gross fixed assets are in `B9`.
- First-year CapEx is in cell `C7` on the CapEx and depreciation schedule.
- Closing gross fixed assets are calculated in `C9`.

Enter:

`=B9+'CapEx and Depreciation Schedule'!C7`

Use the actual worksheet name in your workbook.

Copy the formula across the forecast years.

Additional adjustments may be needed for asset disposals, impairments or other asset movements.

## 2. Forecast Accumulated Depreciation

Accumulated depreciation is generally shown as a deduction from gross fixed assets.

If accumulated depreciation is represented as a negative balance, a simplified formula is:

Closing Accumulated Depreciation = Opening Accumulated Depreciation − Current-Year Depreciation

Suppose:
- Opening accumulated depreciation is in `B10`.
- Current-year depreciation and amortisation is in cell `C13` on the CapEx and depreciation schedule.

Enter:

`=B10-'CapEx and Depreciation Schedule'!C13`

Copy the formula across the forecast years.

Make sure the sign convention is consistent throughout the model.

## 3. Calculate Net Fixed Assets

Net fixed assets represent gross fixed assets after accumulated depreciation.

If gross fixed assets are in row 9 and accumulated depreciation is in row 10, enter:

`=SUM(C9:C10)`

This works when accumulated depreciation is stored as a negative number.

Copy the formula across the forecast years.

## 4. Calculate Total Assets

Once the asset balances have been forecast, update total assets.

For example, if the relevant asset accounts are in cells `C5:C12`, enter:

`=SUM(C5:C12)`

Use the actual range for the asset section of your workbook.

Copy the formula across the forecast years.

## 5. Understand the Cash Balance

Cash cannot be forecast accurately from the fixed asset schedule alone.

It is normally calculated using the cash flow statement.

A simplified formula is:

Closing Cash = Opening Cash + Net Cash Flow

The cash flow statement combines operating, investing and financing cash flows.

Until the cash flow statement has been completed, the balance sheet may not balance.

## Key Takeaways

- Gross fixed assets increase with CapEx, subject to disposals and other adjustments.
- Accumulated depreciation tracks depreciation recognised over time.
- Net fixed assets equal gross fixed assets plus accumulated depreciation when accumulated depreciation is stored as a negative balance.
- Total assets must include all relevant asset accounts.
- Cash is linked to the cash flow statement in a three-statement model.
